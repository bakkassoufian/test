## Full description

### Stop starting every post from zero.

You know you should be posting consistently. You've probably tried AI to help. But every session starts the same way: a blank screen, a vague prompt, and a generic answer you end up rewriting from scratch.

The problem isn't AI. It's the lack of a system around it.

### The AI Content Creator Starter Kit gives you that system.

It's a practical toolkit of 10 PDF files that shows you how to use AI for content creation — and then gives you everything you need to actually do it: copy-ready prompts, adaptable content ideas, proven hook structures, slide-by-slide templates and a 30-day calendar that links each day to the exact prompt, idea or template to use.

AI does the heavy lifting. You add what only you can: your experience, your opinions and your voice.

### What's inside

✅ **01 — Quick Start Guide** (18 pages)
How AI fits into content creation, the 5-step content system, how to write better prompts, a reusable prompt formula, content pillars, platform guide, hooks, CTAs, editing AI output, weekly workflow and your action plan.

✅ **02 — 100 AI Prompts**
Copy-ready prompts in 8 categories: content ideas, Instagram posts, Reels and short videos, TikTok, LinkedIn, captions, storytelling and repurposing. Every prompt includes customization fields.

✅ **03 — 100 Content Ideas**
Adaptable ideas across 8 categories — educational, personal, engagement, inspirational, behind the scenes, problem/solution, lists and promotional — each with a suggested format and CTA.

✅ **04 — 50 Powerful Hooks**
Fill-in-the-blank opening lines in 8 styles, each with a worked example from a different niche.

✅ **05 — 30-Day Content Calendar**
A balanced month of content for one person. Each day includes the idea, format, content pillar, goal, CTA and the toolkit references to use. Includes a setup worksheet, a 30-day tracker and a monthly review.

✅ **06 — Social Media Templates**
15 templates (quote, tips, mistakes, checklist, educational carousel, before/after, FAQ, myth vs fact, step-by-step, announcement, product promotion, testimonial, behind the scenes, storytelling, problem → solution) with slide structure, suggested copy, visual direction and CTA — easy to rebuild in Canva.

✅ **07 — Publishing Checklist**
Before-publishing and after-publishing checklists, final technical checks, a format size reference and a weekly review worksheet.

🎁 **BONUS 08 — 50 CTAs** for comments, saves, shares, follows, website visits, products, leads and community.

🎁 **BONUS 09 — 30 AI Image Prompts** for on-brand visuals across 10 styles, from product photography to editorial.

🎁 **BONUS 10 — Content Repurposing System** — turn one core idea into 16 pieces of content, with 8 copy-ready prompts and a worked example.

### Who it's for

- Beginner content creators who want a clear starting point
- Freelancers and consultants building a personal brand
- Small business owners who need to show up online without a marketing team
- Entrepreneurs launching or growing an offer
- Students building an online presence or portfolio

- Anyone who wants to use AI to create content faster — without sounding like AI

### How it works

1. **Download** the 10 PDF files instantly after purchase.
2. **Read the Quick Start Guide** (about 30 minutes) to understand the system.
3. **Fill in the "Set up your month" worksheet** — your niche, audience and offer.
4. **Follow the 30-day calendar.** Each day points you to the prompt, idea, hook or template to use.
5. **Review every week** with the publishing checklist and keep improving.

### Why it's useful

- **No blank page.** Every day of the month already has a plan.
- **Works with the AI tools you already use.** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and most AI image generators. No extra subscriptions required.
- **Built for real people, not robots.** The guide shows you how to edit AI output so your content sounds like you.
- **Beginner friendly.** Clear explanations, practical examples, no jargon.
- **Reusable.** Run the prompts again with new topics and use the calendar structure month after month.

### What you'll receive

- 10 professionally designed PDF files (A4), organized and numbered
- Instant digital download
- Printable worksheets, checklists and trackers
- Lifetime access to the files you purchased

### Good to know

- This is a digital product. Nothing will be shipped.
- AI tools are not included; the prompts work with free and paid versions of most AI chat assistants.
- Results depend on your niche, effort and consistency. This kit gives you a structure and tools — it doesn't promise followers or sales.
