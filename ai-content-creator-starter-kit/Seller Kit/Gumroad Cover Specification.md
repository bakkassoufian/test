# Gumroad Cover & Preview Specification

Ready-made images are in `images/`. They were rendered from the actual PDF pages, so every mockup shows real product content. Use them as they are, or rebuild them in Canva with the specification below.

## Visual identity

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink (background) | `#0F1013` | Covers, dark panels |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Headlines on dark |
| Off-white | `#F6F5F1` | Light panels |
| Electric violet (accent) | `#5B4CFF` | Badges, rules, highlights |
| Soft violet | `#A99FFF` | Second line of titles |
| Muted grey | `#8D8F99` | Small labels |
| Headline font | Space Grotesk Bold | Titles |
| Body font | Inter Regular / Medium | Subtitles, lists |
| Label font | JetBrains Mono (uppercase, wide letter-spacing) | Small labels, badges |

In Canva, close alternatives are **Space Grotesk** (available in Canva), **Inter** and a monospace font such as **Roboto Mono**.

Background details used throughout: a faint 48 px grid of white lines at 3–4% opacity, and one soft violet radial glow in a corner. Nothing else. Keep it minimal.
