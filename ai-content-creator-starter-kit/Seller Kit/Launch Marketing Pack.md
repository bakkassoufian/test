# Launch Marketing Pack

Ten promotional posts and five Reel ideas to launch the **AI Content Creator Starter Kit** on Gumroad.

**How to use this pack**

- Spread the posts over two to three weeks. Roughly half of them teach something useful from the kit, and the other half talk about the kit directly.
- Replace `[LINK]` with your Gumroad link, or say "link in bio" on Instagram and TikTok.
- Every claim here is something the product actually contains. Keep it that way: no invented sales numbers, testimonials or results. Once real buyers send feedback, ask their permission and share it.

## 5 Reel Ideas

### Reel A: "Stop asking AI for 'an Instagram post'"

**Hook (on screen + spoken):** "If your AI content sounds generic, it's probably your prompt."

**Script (30–35 seconds):**
> "If your AI content sounds generic, it's probably your prompt.
> Most people type: 'Give me an Instagram post.'
> And they get… an Instagram post. For anyone. About anything.
> Instead, tell it who it is, who it's for, what the goal is and what format you want.
> Like this: [read the coffee-shop example quickly].
> Same tool. Completely different result.
> I put 100 prompts like this in my AI Content Creator Starter Kit, so you don't have to write them yourself."

**Visual:** Talking head, then a screen recording showing both prompts typed into an AI chat with the two answers side by side. On-screen text labels: "Bad prompt" and "Better prompt".

**CTA:** "Link in bio if you want all 100."

---

### Reel B: Flip-through

**Hook (on-screen text):** "Everything inside my $9 content toolkit, in 20 seconds."

**Script:** No voiceover. A quick cut every 1.5–2 seconds through the 10 PDF files, with an on-screen label on each: "18-page Quick Start Guide" → "100 AI prompts" → "100 content ideas" → "50 hooks" → "30-day calendar" → "15 templates" → "Publishing checklist" → "Bonus: 50 CTAs" → "Bonus: 30 image prompts" → "Bonus: repurposing system".

**Visual:** Screen recording of scrolling through the PDFs on a laptop or tablet, or printed pages flipped by hand on a desk. Use calm background music.

**CTA (final frame):** "Link in bio."

### Reel C: "What I'd post for the next 7 days"

**Hook:** "If I were starting from zero, this is what I'd post this week."

**Script (40–45 seconds):**
> "If I were starting from zero, this is what I'd post this week.
> Day 1: introduce yourself: who you help and what you'll share.
> Day 2: a beginner's glossary carousel for your niche.
> Day 3: a this-or-that poll in Stories.
> Day 4: a Reel about one common mistake and the fix.
> Day 5: why you started.
> Day 6: a quick win your audience can try in 10 minutes.
> Day 7: review what worked and reply to everyone.
> That's Week 1 of the 30-day calendar in my starter kit. Weeks 2 to 4 are in there too."

**Visual:** Talking head with each day appearing as on-screen text, or a slow pan across the printed Week 1 calendar page.

**CTA:** "Save this and start Monday. Full calendar linked in bio."

### Reel E: One idea, a week of content

**Hook:** "I turned one idea into a week of content in about an hour. Here's how."

> Only use this hook after you've actually done it, and say how long it really took you.

**Script (45–50 seconds):**
> "I turned one idea into a week of content. Here's how.
> The idea: [your topic].
> First, I asked AI to turn it into a brief: the main message, five key points and ten hooks.
> From the brief, I made three short videos, a carousel, a checklist post, five Stories and a LinkedIn post.
> The trick is to change the format and the angle each time, not just copy-paste.
> I wrote the whole system down, with the exact prompts, in the bonus file of my starter kit."

**Visual:** A split screen or quick cuts showing the brief in an AI chat, then each finished piece (video thumbnail, carousel, Story frames, LinkedIn post) appearing one after another.

**CTA:** "Want the system? Link in bio."
