## Step by step on Gumroad

1. Go to **Products → New product**.
2. Choose **E-book** (or **Digital product**), enter the name and price below, and click **Next: Customize**.
3. Paste the **description**, add the **cover** and **thumbnail**, and fill in **Summary** and **Additional details**.
4. Set **URL** to `first-digital-product`.
5. On the **Content** tab, upload the PDF.
6. Add a **discount code** for launch (see below).
7. Click **Publish**, then do a test purchase with a 100% discount code to check the download.

## Description

> Copy everything between the lines. Use Gumroad's editor to make the headings bold.

**You know something people would pay to learn. This guide shows you how to turn it into a product and sell it.**

Most people never launch a digital product. That's not because they lack skills. They don't know where to start, they wait until it's "perfect", or they don't know how to find buyers.

*Your First Digital Product* is a short, practical guide that takes you from "I have no idea" to "my product is live", one step at a time.

**This is for you if:**

✓ You want to sell your first e-book, template, preset or guide
✓ You have skills or experience but don't know how to package them
✓ You don't have a big audience (or any audience yet)
✓ You want a clear plan instead of scattered advice

**What's inside:**

• **Find your idea**: three places to look, a simple formula to make your idea specific, and a scoring filter to pick the best one
• **Choose your format**: e-book, template, checklist, preset, code or course, and which is fastest for you
• **Validate before you build**: how to check demand and build a waitlist with a free product
• **Create your product**: outline, write and finish it, with a quality checklist
• **Set your price**: price on value, and how to use Gumroad's pricing options
• **Build a product page that sells**: name formula, cover tips, and a description template you can copy
• **Launch it**: a 3-phase launch plan, with a launch email template
• **Market without a big audience**: communities, content, email list, search and collaborations
• **Grow to a full store**: product ladder, bundles, upsells and your second product
• **30-day action plan**: a day-by-day checklist from idea to launch
• **3 printable worksheets**: Idea Finder, Product Page Builder and Launch Checklist

**After reading, you'll be able to:**

→ Pick a product idea you can finish in two weeks
→ Write a product page that explains clearly why someone should buy
→ Launch with a plan instead of hoping for sales

**FAQ**

**What format is it?** A 22-page PDF (A4) that you can read on any device or print.
**Is it for complete beginners?** Yes. No experience, website or audience needed.
**Does it promise income?** No. It's an honest, practical guide. Results depend on your product and the work you put in.
